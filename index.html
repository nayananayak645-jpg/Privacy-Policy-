This Privacy Policy explains how the Dr. Preeti Samal mobile app (package name com.doctorpritisamla.app, the “App”) collects, uses, stores and shares your information. The App is operated by Dr. Preeti Samal – MBBS, MS (O & G), Obstetrician & Gynaecologist, Laparoscopic Surgeon (Regd. No. 21888 (O)), at Divine Health Care, Jajpur, Odisha (“we”, “us”, “our”). By using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

The App lets patients request and manage clinic appointments, view their appointment history and prescriptions, receive clinic notifications, and read health tips and clinic information.

1. Information we collect

a) Information you give us

  • Name (asked when you first open the App).
  • Contact and profile details you enter in Appointment Settings or when booking: mobile number, email address, age, gender, address.
  • Appointment details: preferred date and time slot, a description of your health problem, and any additional notes you write.
  • Language preference (English, Hindi or Odia).

b) Information created while you use the App

  • Appointment status (pending, confirmed, completed, cancelled, rejected) and notes added by the clinic.
  • Prescriptions, diagnosis and medicine details issued to you by the doctor.
  • Notifications sent to you by the clinic and whether you have read them.

c) Information collected automatically

  • Anonymous user ID. The App signs you in anonymously through Google Firebase Authentication. This creates a random ID that links your data to your device’s app installation. There is no username or password.
  • Push notification ID (OneSignal subscription/player ID) and basic device information needed to deliver notifications.
  • Advertising ID and basic device and app information used by Google AdMob to show ads (see Section 4).

d) What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, SMS, or payment card details through the App.

2. Health information

The problem description, prescriptions, diagnosis and notes are health-related personal information. We use it only to provide care and manage your appointments. It is visible to the doctor and authorised clinic staff. We do not share your health information with advertisers, and it is not used to select or personalise ads.

3. How we use your information

  • To create, confirm, reschedule or cancel your appointments.
  • To contact you about your appointment, prescription or follow-up.
  • To show your appointment history and prescriptions in the App.
  • To send push notifications about appointments and clinic updates.
  • To show you ads, which help us keep the App available.
  • To keep the App secure, fix problems and improve it.
  • To meet legal and medical record-keeping obligations.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob: banner ads inside some screens and a full-screen ad that may appear when you tap “Book Appointment”. AdMob may use your device’s advertising ID and device/app data to deliver and measure ads, and may show personalised ads.

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads; the path may differ on your phone).
  • Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • We are not responsible for the content of third-party ads. Do not rely on any ad as medical advice.

6. Storage, transfer and security

Your data is stored on Google Cloud (Firebase) servers, which may be located outside India. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections, and access to records is restricted using security rules and authorised clinic accounts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Because the App uses anonymous sign-in, your data is linked to this app installation. If you uninstall the App, clear its data or change phone, you may lose access to your history in the App. The clinic may still hold your records as described below.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide care, manage the clinic and meet legal or medical record-keeping duties. Medical records may need to be kept for a period required by law even after a deletion request.

To request deletion of your data, email us at Preetisamal400@gmail.com from the email you used in the App (or tell us the name and mobile number you entered) with the subject “Delete my data”. We will respond within 30 days and tell you what we deleted and what we must legally retain.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit your details in Appointment Settings, and ask us to correct anything else.
  • Deletion: as described in Section 7.
  • Notifications: turn them off any time in your phone’s app notification settings.
  • Ads: reset or opt out of ad personalisation as described in Section 4.
  • Withdraw consent: stop using the App and ask us to delete your data.

You may have additional rights under applicable Indian law. To use any right, contact us using Section 11.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. A person under 18 should use the App only with a parent or guardian, who may also book appointments for them. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 without a parent’s consent. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
